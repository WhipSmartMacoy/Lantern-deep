How to use: load a capture from Scene3D (a splat .ply or a solid .glb/.obj/.ply model). Fix the up axis if the object is sideways, then use Keep radius, Cut floor and Auto-crop to remove the room around it. Pick the preset for what you're making, adjust Turn and Zoom until it reads well at game size, and press Download PNG. Send the PNG to Claude and it becomes part of the game. The file name tells the game which sprite it replaces, so keep it.

Splats are frozen in place, so they're ideal for items, props, walls and statues. For walking characters, use an animated .glb model; its clips show up under Animation.
