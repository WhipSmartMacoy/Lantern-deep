LANTERN DEEP - ART FOLDER
=========================

Three ways art gets into the game. Use any mix.

MY DRAWINGS
  Draw at any size (bigger is easier) and save as PNG, or photograph a paper drawing.
  Name the file after the sprite it replaces - the names are under each picture in
  "Current art (reference, 4x size).png". Example: i_sword.png, mob_skel.png, t_wall_0.png
  Claude shrinks it to the game size and puts it in.
  - The camera looks north and down at about 51 degrees. Draw things as seen from the front and a bit above.
  - Use a plain or transparent background.
  - Characters that move need several poses. Start with items, props and tiles; they are single pictures.

SCENE3D CAPTURES
  Save the capture (.ply, .splat or .glb) here, named after the sprite it replaces.
  Good for items, props, rocks, walls. Not for things that walk.

BLENDER
  Models go in Blender\models. Double-click "Render art.bat" to render everything listed in jobs.json
  from the game camera with dark lighting. Pictures land in Blender\renders, then Claude shrinks them
  into sprites and puts them in the game.

Then tell Claude what you added.
